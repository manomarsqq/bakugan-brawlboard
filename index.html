Brawl Board Builder

Build a Bakugan brawl board for your AO3 fic by clicking and typing. No code to write: copy the result into your chapter.

1 Install the Work Skin (once)

  1. On AO3, go to Dashboard → Skins → My Work Skins → Create Work Skin.
  2. Give it any title, paste the Work Skin into the CSS box, and submit.
  3. On your fic's Edit page, pick it under Select Work Skin and post.

Live preview

Click a Bakugan or one of its balls to edit it, a Gate Card space to set a card there, or a brawler’s name or hand to edit their cards. Point at anything to see its info panel, just like readers will.

3 Copy into your chapter

  1. In your chapter's editor on AO3, switch to HTML (not Rich Text).
  2. Paste the board where you want it in the story, then preview or post.
Edit a board you made before

Paste a board's code here (for example, copied back out of your chapter's HTML) to keep editing it, such as for the next round.

Your board is saved in this browser as you work. The names of cards still in a hand or set face-down are hidden from readers, but stay in the board's HTML so you can load it back; leave a name empty to keep it out.
